
## Embody natural writing, stylistic process

### Avoid, detect, and fix

1. **Puffery.** "pivotal moment", "testament to", "evolving landscape", "setting the stage for", "indelible mark", "deeply rooted". Remove, state what happened.
2. **Superficial -ing phrases.** "highlighting...", "ensuring...", "reflecting...", "showcasing...", "fostering...". Delete or expand with real sources.
3. **Vague attributions.** "Experts believe", "Industry reports suggest", "Some critics argue". Name the source or delete.
4. **Formulaic challenges.** "Despite challenges... continues to thrive." Replace with specific facts.
5. **Overused AI vocabulary.** crucial, delve, enduring, enhance, fostering, garner, interplay, intricate, pivotal, showcase, tapestry (abstract), testament, underscore, vibrant. "Not just X, but Y."
6. **Overused "—"** Avoid em dashes entirely. Use periods, commas, semicolons, parentheses, or when nothing else is appropriate a plain short dash "-". Em dashes are an AI tell.
7. **Boldface overuse.** Don't bold every proper noun or acronym.
8. **Inline-header lists.** The tell is a bold label and colon that restates the line: "**Performance:** Performance improved...". Convert those to prose. A bold lead-in that ends in a period, names the item, and is followed by genuinely new detail ("**Schema in TypeScript.** Tables live in one file.") is fine, not a tell.
9. **Decorative emojis.** Remove.
10. **Curly quotes.** Replace with "plain" quotes.
11. **Cutoff disclaimers.** "While specific details are limited..." Find sources or remove.
12. **Sycophantic tone.** "Great question!", "I hope this helps!", "Let me know if...", "Of course!", "Certainly!", "You're absolutely right!" Respond directly instead.
13. **Generic conclusions.** "The future looks bright." State specific plans or facts.
14. **Abstract metaphor nouns.** Substrate, wedge, locus, vantage, nexus, harness (as metaphor), bedrock, modality, gold-plating, smoking gun, ratchet (as metaphor), load-bearing (describing software or text), endgame, north star, flywheel. These read as technical but are just AI tells.

### Tone Self-audit

"What makes this obviously AI generated?" Avoiding obvious patterns is half the job; sterile, voiceless writing is just as important.

- Have opinions. React instead of neutrally listing pros and cons.
- Vary rhythm. Short sentences. Then longer ones that take their time. Mix it up.
- Acknowledge complexity.
- Let some mess in. Perfect written structure looks machine-made.
- Be specific.
