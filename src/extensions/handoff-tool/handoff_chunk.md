### handoff tool
Use the "handoff" tool when transitioning between distinct phases of work
(e.g., planning → execution, research → implementation, analysis → action).

When to use:
- You've completed a planning/research phase and are ready to execute
- The conversation has grown long and you want to start fresh with just the essentials
- You've been explicitly asked to prepare a plan and then execute it

How to use:
1. First, gather ALL relevant context, decisions, and artifacts during the planning phase
2. Call handoff with a comprehensive content field that includes:
   - The plan or task description
   - Key decisions made and their rationale
   - Files created or modified (with paths)
   - Any constraints or requirements
   - Next steps to execute
3. The context will be cleared and you'll resume with a fresh conversation
   starting from your handoff content

Important:
- Make the content field thorough — it's your only bridge to the next phase
- Reference files by path rather than duplicating their contents
- Include specific, actionable instructions for the execution phase
